## Findings after rerunning

The old positive headline is not retained. Large absolute graph scores occur in both populations. A small detour contrast appears in two cohorts and is reported; the additional sensitivity cohort does not resolve it. A later interval containing zero is not treated as proof of equality.

Held-out selected-bit and equal-budget search intervals include zero. The fixed six-bit cube comparison gives no resolved advantage under its stated controls. Input entry and exact register transport explain the controlled trailing-register timing pattern. Actual digest classifiers average near one half under the fixed models.

The original protocol contains an unused search.discovery_bases setting. The candidate was fixed from prior research; the executed search evaluates 256 new bases. No separate search-discovery experiment is claimed.
